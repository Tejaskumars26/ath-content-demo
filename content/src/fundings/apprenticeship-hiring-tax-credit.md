## About the credit

Employers can claim a credit for each apprentice who completes at least six months in a registered program.

:::largeCallout{ showHeader="true" calloutType="informational" }

You must keep payroll records for each apprentice for at least three years.

:::

## How to claim

Submit the apprenticeship certificate with your annual business tax return.
