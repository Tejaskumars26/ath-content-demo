## Who can apply

Businesses of any size can apply if they plan to install equipment that reduces energy use by at least 15%.

1. Request an energy assessment
2. Choose qualifying equipment from the approved list
3. Submit your loan application with the assessment report

:::largeCallout{ showHeader="true" headerText="Loan terms" calloutType="quickReference" amountIconText="$25,000 to $500,000" filingTypeIconText="" frequencyIconText="" phoneIconText="" phoneIconLabel="" emailIconText="" }

Loans from $25,000 to $500,000 with terms of up to ten years and a fixed interest rate.

:::
