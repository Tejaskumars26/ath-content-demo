## Eligibility

Your business must be located in a designated downtown district and have fewer than **50 employees**. You must own or lease the building you want to improve.

* The business has operated for at least one year
* The improvements are visible from the street
* Work has not started before your application is approved

:::largeCallout{ showHeader="true" headerText="Benefits" calloutType="conditional" amountIconText="Up to $20,000" filingTypeIconText="" frequencyIconText="" phoneIconText="" phoneIconLabel="" emailIconText="" }

Grants of up to $20,000 to cover new signs, lighting, windows, doors, and exterior painting.

:::

## How to apply

Prepare photos of the current storefront and a quote from a licensed contractor. Then submit your application through the [online portal](https://www.example.gov/apply/main-street).

:::largeCallout{ showHeader="true" calloutType="warning" }

Applications are reviewed in the order they are received. Funding is limited each year.

:::
