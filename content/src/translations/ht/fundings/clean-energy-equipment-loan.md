## Ki moun ki ka aplike

Biznis tout gwosè ka aplike si yo gen plan pou enstale ekipman ki diminye konsomasyon enèji omwen 15%.

1. Mande yon evalyasyon enèji
2. Chwazi ekipman ki kalifye nan lis apwouve a
3. Soumèt aplikasyon prè ou a ak rapò evalyasyon an

:::largeCallout{ showHeader="true" headerText="Loan terms" calloutType="quickReference" amountIconText="$25,000 to $500,000" filingTypeIconText="" frequencyIconText="" phoneIconText="" phoneIconLabel="" emailIconText="" }

Prè ant $25,000 ak $500,000 ak yon tèm ki rive jiska dis ane epi yon to enterè fiks.

:::
